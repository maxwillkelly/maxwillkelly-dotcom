I work as a Software Engineer on [GovernorHub](https://governorhub.com/), the leading online service for school governors and trustees.

I have become the most senior developer on the team. In addition to building software, I've had the opportunity to onboard and train new engineers. I work with product managers and other stakeholders to write technical specifications and gather requirements.

My colleagues and I were responsible for a complete ground up rewrite of GovernorHub's front-end. At the time I joined, GovernorHub was a legacy [AngularJS](https://angularjs.org/) codebase with [React](https://react.dev/) injected into it for specific components. We completely rearchitected it using the [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) App router which produced a much better user experience. I was responsible for reimplementing many significant features in [React](https://react.dev/).

I built processes to triage bugs from customer feedback in [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) and convert them into issues in Linear. I've worked on integrations with products maintained by other teams internally and externally such as [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/) and [Get Information About Schools (GIAS)](https://get-information-schools.service.gov.uk/).

I've also had the opportunity to onboard new developers into an expanding team and write developer specifications for new features.
